# Executive Summary – US Top 50 Playlist Analysis
**Scope:** 555 daily snapshots (2024-05-18 → 2025-11-27), 977 unique songs, 244 artists.

**Headlines**
- **Stable chart:** only ~3.5 of 50 slots change per day; 66% of songs last <14 days, but 35 songs held ≥180 days.
- **Concentrated market:** the top 5 artists capture 27.0% of all chart "points".
- **Singles win:** avg rank 24.0 vs 26.1 for album tracks; Top-10 share 25% vs 18%.
- **Explicit content is neutral:** 48% of slots; same average rank as clean tracks.
- **Popularity ≠ rank:** correlation is only ρ = -0.25; use longevity & volatility alongside it.

**Actions:** prioritise single-led releases, fund sustained promotion (not just launch week), focus-track large albums, and use seasonal windows for catalog re-entry.
